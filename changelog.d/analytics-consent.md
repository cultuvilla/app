- Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) are now opt-in, as the
  Privacy Policy says: off for guests and for every account until it says yes.
  Signing up no longer asks: a full-screen page — «¿Nos ayudas a mejorar
  Cultuvilla?», with «No, gracias» and «Sí, claro» side by side — appears after
  an account's first real action (signing up to an event, joining a group or a
  pueblo, publishing something) or on the third day it opens the app, whichever
  comes first. Closed with the back button, it asks once more another day and
  then never again. The answer can be changed anytime in Ajustes →
  «Estadísticas de uso». Error reports keep reaching us either way.
  **Migration:** none — an account with no answer stored simply has not been
  asked yet.
