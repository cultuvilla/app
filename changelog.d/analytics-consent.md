- Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) are now opt-in, as the
  Privacy Policy says: off for guests and for every account until it says yes.
  New accounts answer «¿Nos ayudas a mejorar Cultuvilla con estadísticas de uso
  anónimas?» while completing their profile, with neither answer preselected;
  existing accounts see the same question once, as a sheet. The answer can be
  changed anytime in Ajustes → «Estadísticas de uso». Error reports keep
  reaching us either way. **Migration:** none — an account with no answer
  stored simply has not been asked yet.
